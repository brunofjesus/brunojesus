Title: New keyboards!
Date: Mon, 12 Mar 2018 22:50:00 +0000
---

For a long time I was using the good old Cherry G80-3000 keyboards, they are really good keyboards and still working great, but I find the old cherry MX Black switches to be kinda heavy.

![Cherry G8 3000](/assets/media/20180312_newkeyboards/cherry_g80_3000.jpg)

I wanted some new keyboards to replace them but I didn't feel like spending a ton of money, after some searching on [Gearbest](https://gearbest.com) I found the [Ajazz AK33 Keyboard](https://www.gearbest.com/keyboards/pp_687881.html?wid=21) with a clone of the Cherry MX Blue switches.

![Ajazz AK33](/assets/media/20180312_newkeyboards/ajazz_ak33.jpg)

The AK33 is very different from the G80-3000, the switches are lighter (and also a bit louder), doesn't have a numpad, and it has **backlight**!

I thought the biggest issue will be the transition from the Portuguese layout to the US layout, after some time writing I got used to it, I use the EurKEY layout, so if I want to write in Portuguese I just need a few key combinations for the accentuation.

The keycaps are fine and build quality is really impressive for a 30€ mechanical keyboard.

That's it, if you want a cheap mechanical keyboard I think this is a great choice!
